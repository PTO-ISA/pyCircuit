# Code of Conduct

pyCircuit contributors and maintainers are expected to keep collaboration
respectful, technical, and constructive in repository discussions, reviews,
and related community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained disruption.

## Reporting

For routine moderation, ask a maintainer privately through an existing PTO-ISA
maintainer channel. If no private channel is available, open a private
[repository security advisory](https://github.com/PTO-ISA/pyCircuit/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Conduct report:`. Do not disclose sensitive conduct
reports in public issues or pull requests.

Maintainers will limit report details to people needed for review, consider the
context and severity, and may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must
not adjudicate it.
