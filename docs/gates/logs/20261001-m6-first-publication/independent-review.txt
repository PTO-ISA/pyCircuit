linked final, CPP bundle, and RTL bundle at the five reachable checkpoints.
`after_previous_saved` is excluded as unreachable for `had_previous=false`.
Every crash asserts the exact point marker, `-SIGKILL`, journal phase and owner,
stage/destination/previous bytes, control owner bytes, and stable lock identity.

Linked-final precommit recovery is observed through public `emit`: it must fail
with the specific missing stable-artifact diagnostic, leave the final and emit
output absent, and clear the transaction before a clean first republish. The
committed row preserves the expected final bytes and emits the expected CPP
bundle.

`after_journal_preparing`. The test inspects the recovered state before any new
stage is built: absent destination for four precommit rows and the original
committed bytes for the commit row, with no stage/previous and unchanged
control/lock identity. It then SIGKILLs that paused writer and proves a third
public writer recovers and republishes the expected bundle bytes. This adds ten
pause/kill/recovery sequences without counting them as the 15 matrix rows.
