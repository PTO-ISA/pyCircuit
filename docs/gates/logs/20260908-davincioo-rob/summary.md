  duplicate completion/ack, exactly-once drain;
- backpressure: allocation output and committed output retain transactions and
  resume after host consumption;
- invalid: unused slot, generation/epoch/flow/instruction/block mismatches,
  inactive inputs, repeated completion, non-durable/partial/mismatched acks;
- recovery: flush competes with completion, logical epoch invalidation, stale
  recovery, irrevocable published-but-unconsumed output, recovery allocation
  stall, old-epoch ack, late completion, resume;
- conflicts: flush/completion/ack offered together, reliable ack after flush,
  two structurally bound flows, cross-flow rejection and independent state.

Reset images are explicitly checked before recording. Four-bit slot identity
has no representable out-of-range index; the invalid-slot case addresses an
unallocated in-range row. Finite generation/epoch wrap constraints are in rob.md.
